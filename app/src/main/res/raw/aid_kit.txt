Check off what is in your kit. Restock the rest before you go.
# Wound care
- Nitrile gloves, 2 pairs or more
- Sterile gauze pads, 4x4 inch
- Rolled gauze
- Adhesive bandages, assorted
- Cloth tape, 1 inch
- Irrigation syringe, 10 to 20 ml
- Antiseptic wipes, for skin around a wound and for tools
- Moleskin or blister pads
# Bleeding
- Tourniquet, commercial, windlass type
- Trauma dressing, 5x9 inch
- Hemostatic gauze, for packing a deep wound
# Bone and joint
- SAM splint
- Elastic bandage, 3 or 4 inch
- Triangular bandages, 2
# Medicines
- Ibuprofen and acetaminophen
- Antihistamine
- Aspirin, chewable: 1 regular (325 mg) or 4 low-dose (81 mg), for chest pain in adults only. See Chest pain.
- Oral rehydration salts
- Glucose tablets or gel
- Your own epinephrine (auto-injector or nasal spray), 2 doses
- Your own inhaler
- Your own prescriptions, extra days
# Tools
- Trauma shears
- Tweezers, fine point
- Safety pins
- Headlamp, spare batteries
- Whistle
- Emergency blanket
- Notebook and pencil for a SOAP note
# Upkeep
- Check expiry dates before each trip
- Replace anything opened, wet or used
+++
# Notes
- Size it to the group and days out.
- Everyone carries their own epinephrine, inhaler and prescriptions, and says where. See Stings and anaphylaxis.
- A commercial tourniquet beats an improvised one. See Severe bleeding.
- Write times, vital signs and what you did in the notebook, for rescuers. See Scene size-up and primary assessment.
- Waterproof bag, top of the pack, and the group knows where.
