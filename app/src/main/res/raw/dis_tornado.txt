! Tornado warning for where you are: take shelter now. Don't wait to see or hear it; it may be hidden by rain or dark.
# Now
1. Go to a basement or safe room. No basement: a small room in the middle of the lowest floor, such as a closet, bathroom or inner hallway. Put as many walls as you can between you and the outside. Keep away from windows.
2. Get under something sturdy, like a heavy table. Close the doors.
3. Protect your head and neck: a bicycle or motorcycle helmet, pillows, heavy coats, blankets or a quilt.
4. Don't stop to open or close windows. It does not save the house; use the seconds to get to shelter.
# Not at home
- School or work: go to the tornado shelter. Not a gym, cafeteria or auditorium.
- Mobile home, tent, shed: not safe. Leave well before the storm for a sturdy building.
- Outside: get into a sturdy building. None near: lie flat in a ditch or low spot and cover your head with your hands.
- In a vehicle: drive to the nearest shelter. If you can't, either get down in the car and cover your head, or leave it for a ditch or low spot.
- Never shelter under a highway overpass. Wind speeds up there.
+++
# Watch and warning
- A tornado watch means a tornado could form. Get ready and keep listening.
- A tornado warning means one has been seen or found on radar. Take shelter.
- Keep NOAA Weather Radio, local radio or TV on.
