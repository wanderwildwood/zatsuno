! Stuck in a car: stay with it. Leave it and you can be lost within minutes in blowing snow and cold.
# Stuck in a car
1. Stay in the car. Call or text where you are if you can (see Calling for help).
2. Run the engine about 10 minutes each hour for heat. While it runs, open a window a little and keep the exhaust pipe clear of snow. Carbon monoxide kills.
3. Be seen: the dome light on at night while the engine runs, a bright cloth (red if you can) tied to the antenna or door, and the hood up once the snow stops.
4. Now and then move your arms, legs, fingers and toes hard. Don't overdo it: shoveling or pushing a car in the cold can bring on a heart attack, and sweat chills you.
5. Drink: melt snow first. Eating snow cools you.
# Outside, no shelter
- Stay dry and cover all bare skin.
- Make a windbreak, lean-to or snow cave. Light a fire for heat and to be seen; rocks around it hold and give back heat.
# Home with no heat
- Close off rooms you don't need. Towels under doors, curtains shut.
- Eat, and drink water. Loose layers; take one off before you sweat.
- Fireplace, wood stove or heater: keep it vented. Keep the furnace vent clear of drifts. See Power outage.
+++
# Driving in a storm
- Slow down. Wet-looking roads can be ice.
- Clear all snow and ice off the car first. Tell someone your route. Take a charged phone, a charger and a kit.
- Skidding: ease off the gas and steer where you want the front to go. With ABS, press the brake steady; don't pump it.
- Can't see: pull right off and stop. Lights off and the parking brake on, so no one follows your lights into you.
- Cold and wet: see Hypothermia or Frostbite.
