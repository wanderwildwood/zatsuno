title: Burns
keywords: burn, scald, fire, flame, stove, hot water, blister, electrical burn, sunburn, chemical
source: MedlinePlus (NIH), "Burns".
---
! Call 911 for burns that are large, deep, charred or white, on the face, hands, feet, groin or a joint, or from electricity or chemicals.
# On fire
- Stop, drop and roll, or smother the flames with a coat or blanket, then pour on water.
# Do this
1. Get them away from the heat. With electricity, turn off the power first or move them with something that isn't metal.
2. Cool the burn under cool running water, not ice, for 5 to 30 minutes.
3. Take off rings, watches and tight things before it swells. Leave clothing that is stuck to the skin.
4. Cover loosely with a clean, dry, non-stick dressing or cloth.
5. A large burn: keep them warm and watch for shock.
# Don't
- No butter, oil, ice, toothpaste or home remedies.
- Don't break blisters.
> Cooling with water, and nothing on a serious burn but a clean cover, is the current advice (MedlinePlus). Butter and ice are old remedies that make burns worse.
