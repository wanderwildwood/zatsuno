# Signs
- Thirst, dry mouth, tiredness, headache
- Little urine, or urine dark yellow or amber
- Dizziness, especially on standing up
! Call 911 for confusion, fainting, a fast heartbeat or fast breathing, no urine for many hours, or signs of heat stroke.
# Do this
1. Rest in shade.
2. Sip water often, or suck ice. Small amounts keep down better than large ones.
3. After heavy sweating, vomiting or diarrhea, water alone is not enough. Oral rehydration salts mixed with safe water put back what was lost; a sports drink helps after sweating.
4. Keep drinking until the urine is pale again.
# Don't
- With diarrhea, avoid very sweet drinks such as juice or soda. They can make it worse.
- Don't wait to feel thirsty on a hot day. Drink before you need to.
