title: Shock
keywords: pale, clammy, cold sweat, weak pulse, fast breathing, faint, confused, blood loss, low blood pressure
source: MedlinePlus (NIH), "Shock".
---
! Call 911 or your local emergency number as soon as you see signs of shock. Do not wait for them to get worse.
# Signs
- Pale or gray, cool, clammy skin
- Fast, weak pulse; fast, shallow breathing
- Anxious, confused, drowsy or faint
- Thirst, nausea, vomiting
# Do this
1. Treat the cause you can: stop bleeding first.
2. Lay them flat on their back.
3. If there is no injury to the head, neck, back or legs, raise the feet about 12 inches (30 cm).
4. Cover them to keep them warm, including underneath if the ground is cold. Loosen tight clothing.
5. Stay with them and keep checking breathing.
# Don't
- Don't give anything to eat or drink.
- Don't move them if you think the spine is hurt.
- If they vomit, roll them onto their side. With a spine injury, roll head and body together as one.
