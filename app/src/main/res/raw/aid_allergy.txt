---
! Trouble breathing, a swollen tongue or throat, faintness, or hives with repeated vomiting is anaphylaxis. Give their epinephrine (auto-injector or nasal spray) now. See Stings and anaphylaxis.
! Call 911 or your local emergency number. No signal: try 911 anyway (see Calling for help); if it fails, send someone for help.
- Struggling to breathe: let them sit up.
# A mild reaction
- Itching, sneezing, a local rash or hives, mild swelling
1. Move them away from the cause, if you know it.
2. Wash off any plant or chemical with soap and water.
3. A cold, wet cloth or an antihistamine they already use eases itching.
4. Watch them. Mild can turn severe.
+++
> After epinephrine they need a hospital today. If no help can come, walk out slowly once all signs are gone, with company and the second dose.
# Get medical care if
- It spreads or gets worse
- It came from a medicine
- They have had a severe one before
# Don't
- Don't count on past allergy shots.
- Don't give an antihistamine instead of epinephrine. It is too slow and won't fix breathing.
