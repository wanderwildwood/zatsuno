! Trouble breathing, a swollen tongue or throat, or faintness is anaphylaxis. Call 911 and see Stings and anaphylaxis.
# A mild reaction
- Itchy eyes, sneezing, a local rash or hives, mild swelling where something touched the skin
1. Move them away from whatever caused it, if you know.
2. Wash skin that touched a plant or chemical with soap and water.
3. A cold, wet cloth eases itching. An antihistamine they already use can help.
4. Watch them closely. A mild reaction can turn into a severe one.
# Get medical care if
- The reaction spreads or gets worse
- It came from a medicine
- They have had a severe reaction before
# Don't
- Don't assume earlier allergy shots will protect them.
- Don't put a pillow under the head of someone who is struggling to breathe.
