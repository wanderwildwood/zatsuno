title: Trouble breathing and asthma
keywords: asthma, asthma attack, can't breathe, cannot breathe, short of breath, out of breath, breathless, wheezing, wheeze, tight chest, chest tightness, coughing, gasping, inhaler, puffer, rescue inhaler, albuterol, spacer
source: MedlinePlus (NIH), "Asthma" and "Breathing difficulties"; NHLBI (NIH), "Asthma: Treatment and Action Plan"; AHA/Red Cross Guidelines for First Aid (2024); Wilderness Medical Society Clinical Practice Guidelines.
---
! Can't speak more than a few words, too breathless to walk, blue or gray lips, or drowsy or confused: call 911 or your local emergency number now. No signal: try 911 anyway (see Calling for help); if it fails, send someone for help.
! Swollen lips or tongue, hives, or a sting or food just before: see Stings and anaphylaxis.
! Chest pain or pressure with the breathlessness: see Chest pain.
! At altitude, breathless at rest or a wet cough: go down now, with others helping them. See Altitude sickness.
# Do this
1. Help them sit up, leaning forward. Don't make them lie down or walk until breathing is easy again.
2. Help them use their own reliever inhaler, with a spacer if they have one. Follow their asthma plan or the label.
3. Coach slow breaths: in through the nose, out through pursed lips. Stay with them; your calm helps.
4. Move them away from smoke, dust, cold wind or whatever set it off. In cold air, have them breathe through a scarf or buff.
5. No better in 5 to 10 minutes, or worse: they can repeat the inhaler as their plan says. Call 911 or your local emergency number. No signal: try 911 anyway (see Calling for help); if it fails, send someone for help and stay with them.
6. If they stop breathing normally, start CPR (see Hands-only CPR).
+++
> Guidance (2024) now favors a spacer. No spacer: cut a hole in the base of a clean plastic drink bottle (about 500 mL), fit the mouthpiece in it, and have them breathe from the bottle's neck.
# Head out today if
- It took more than one round of the inhaler
- They have no inhaler, or it is lost, empty or low
- It comes back within a few hours
- They are breathless with no known asthma or other clear cause
