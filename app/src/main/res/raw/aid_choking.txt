! If they can cough hard or speak, let them keep coughing, and stay with them.
# Can't speak, cough or breathe: adult or child over 1
1. Have someone call 911 or your local emergency number.
2. Stand behind them. Hold them across the chest with one arm and lean them forward.
3. Give 5 firm blows between the shoulder blades with the heel of your hand.
4. Then 5 abdominal thrusts: your fist just above the navel, your other hand over it, pull sharply in and up.
5. Keep going, 5 and 5, until the object comes out or they collapse.
- Pregnant, or too big to reach around: thrust on the middle of the breastbone instead.
# Baby under 1
1. Lay the baby face down along your forearm, head lower than the body, jaw held in your fingers.
2. Give 5 firm back blows between the shoulder blades.
3. Turn the baby face up. Give 5 chest thrusts with the heel of one hand on the middle of the breastbone, just below the nipples.
4. Repeat until the object comes out or the baby stops responding.
# If they collapse
1. Lay them on a firm surface and start CPR. Compressions can push the object out.
2. Look in the mouth before any breath. Take the object out only if you can see it.
> MedlinePlus now gives back blows and abdominal thrusts together for adults, and the heel of one hand for a baby's chest thrusts. Older US advice taught abdominal thrusts alone, and two fingers for a baby.
- Anyone who needed thrusts should be seen by a doctor afterward.
