title: Hypothermia
keywords: cold, shivering, wet, exposure, hypothermia, confusion, slurred speech, fumbling, winter, cold water
source: CDC, "Preventing Hypothermia" and "Avoid, Spot, Treat Frostbite and Hypothermia".
---
! Hypothermia is a medical emergency. Call for help. It can happen above 40°F (4°C) when someone is wet.
# Signs
- Shivering, tiredness, clumsy or fumbling hands
- Confusion, memory loss, slurred speech, drowsiness
- Shivering can stop as it gets worse. That is a bad sign, not a good one.
# Do this
1. Get them out of the wind and wet, into shelter.
2. Take off wet clothing.
3. Warm the center of the body first: chest, neck, head and groin. Use dry layers, blankets, a sleeping bag, skin-to-skin contact.
4. Put something between them and the cold ground.
5. If they are awake and can swallow, give warm, sweet drinks. No alcohol.
6. Handle them gently and keep them lying down if they are very cold.
- Not breathing normally: start CPR, and keep warming them.
