# The scene first
1. Stop and look before you go in: traffic, falling rock, moving water, fire, wires, animals.
2. If it is not safe, stay back and call for help from where you are.
3. Put on gloves if you have them.
# Then the person
1. Tap their shoulder and shout. Do they answer?
2. If not, shout for help. Have someone call 911 or your local emergency number.
3. Look at the chest for up to 10 seconds. Gasping is not normal breathing.
- Not breathing normally: start CPR. See Hands-only CPR.
- Breathing: look for heavy bleeding and stop it first. See Severe bleeding.
# A, B, C
- Airway: is it open? Tilt the head back and lift the chin, unless you think the neck is hurt.
- Breathing: does the chest rise and fall?
- Circulation: is there heavy bleeding? Is the skin pale, cold or sweaty?
> For someone who has collapsed and is not breathing normally, chest compressions now come first (C-A-B). The older order began with the airway (A-B-C). NHLBI and MedlinePlus both start with compressions.
# If they are breathing and you must step away
- Roll them onto their side with the top knee bent, so the airway stays clear.
- Do not move someone you think has a neck or back injury unless where they are is dangerous.
- Keep them warm, stay close, and check their breathing every few minutes.
