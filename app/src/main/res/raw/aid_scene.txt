# The scene first
1. Look before you go in: traffic, falling rock, moving water, fire, wires, animals.
2. Not safe: stay back and call from there.
3. Put on gloves if you have them.
# Then the person
1. Tap their shoulder and shout. Do they answer?
2. No answer: shout for help. Have someone call 911 or your local emergency number and bring an AED. Alone: call on speaker and stay. No signal: try 911 anyway (see Calling for help); if it fails, send others.
3. Watch the chest for up to 10 seconds, a full minute if they are very cold (see Hypothermia). Gasping is not normal breathing.
! Not breathing normally, or not sure: start CPR now. See Hands-only CPR.
- Breathing: stop any heavy bleeding first. See Severe bleeding.
# Breathing but not responding
- Neck or back may be hurt: leave them as they lie and keep the airway open.
- Otherwise, or if they vomit, the place is dangerous, or you must leave: roll them onto their side, top knee bent, head, neck and body in a line.
- Keep them warm: something under them as well as over.
- Stay close. Check breathing every few minutes. If it stops or turns to gasping, roll them onto their back and start CPR.
+++
# Breathing: check A, B, C
- Airway: open? Tilt the head back and lift the chin. Neck hurt? Move it as little as you can; the airway still comes first.
- Breathing: does the chest rise and fall?
- Circulation: heavy bleeding? Skin pale, cold or sweaty? See Shock.
> Not breathing normally: compressions come first (C-A-B), before the airway.
