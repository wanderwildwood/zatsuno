Check off what you have. Get the rest before you need it.
# Water and food
- Water: 1 gallon (4 liters) per person per day, for at least 3 days
- Food for 3 days
- Manual can opener
# Light and news
- Flashlight
- Radio, a NOAA Weather Radio if you can
- Extra batteries
- Phone and chargers
# Health
- First-aid kit (see First-aid kit checklist)
- Medicines
- Emergency blanket
# Papers and keys
- Family and emergency contact numbers
- Personal documents
- Cash
- Spare car and house keys
- Maps
+++
> Store 2 weeks of water at home if you can, and more for pregnancy, illness, pets or a hot climate. Change stored water every 6 months.
