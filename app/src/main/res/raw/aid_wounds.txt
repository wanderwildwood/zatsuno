---
! Severe bleeding comes first. If firm pressure doesn't stop it, call 911 or your local emergency number. See Severe bleeding.
# Clean and cover
1. Wash your hands. Gloves on if you have them.
2. Press on it until the bleeding stops.
3. Wash around it with mild soap. Flush the wound hard with at least a quart (1 liter) of drinking water from a squeeze bottle or syringe, even if it looks clean.
4. Pick out dirt you can see. Rinse again.
5. Cover it with a clean dressing that won't stick. Change it daily, or when wet or dirty.
- Large, deep wound: once bleeding is controlled, gently flush out dirt. Don't dig or disturb clots. Cover it and get help.
- Don't pull out an object stuck deep in it: pad around it. Don't push back organs or tissue that stick out: cover them with a clean dressing. Get help.
+++
- No hydrogen peroxide or rubbing alcohol: they harm tissue.
# Get medical care if
- Deep, gaping, on the face, or to the bone (may need stitches).
- Numb past the wound, or a finger or limb won't move right.
- An animal or human bite.
- Infection: spreading redness, warmth, swelling, pus, a red streak, fever.
  Fever, confusion or a red streak: head out now.
- Last tetanus shot over 10 years ago (5 for a dirty, puncture or bite wound), or unknown.
