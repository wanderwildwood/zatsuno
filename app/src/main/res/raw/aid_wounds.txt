! Severe bleeding comes first. See Severe bleeding.
# Clean and cover
1. Wash your hands, and put on gloves if you have them.
2. Press on it until the bleeding stops.
3. Wash the wound with clean water and mild soap. Rinse a puncture under running water for 5 minutes.
4. Pick out dirt you can see. Rinse again.
5. Cover it with a clean dressing that won't stick. Change it daily, or when it gets wet or dirty.
- Even a wound that looks clean gets washed.
- Don't try to clean a large, deep wound. Cover it and get help.
- Don't pull out an object stuck deep in the body. Pad around it and get help.
# Get medical care if
- It is deep, gaping, on the face, or reaches bone. It may need stitches.
- Bleeding doesn't stop after 10 minutes of pressure.
- It was an animal or human bite.
- It shows infection: spreading redness, warmth, swelling, pus, a red streak, fever.
- Their last tetanus shot was more than 10 years ago, or 5 for a dirty wound.
