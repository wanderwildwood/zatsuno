# Remove it now
1. Grip the tick as close to the skin as you can with fine-tipped tweezers. Fingers will do if you have nothing else.
2. Pull it straight out with steady, even pressure. Don't twist or jerk.
3. If mouthparts stay in the skin, leave them. The skin pushes them out as it heals.
4. Clean the bite and your hands with soap and water, rubbing alcohol or sanitizer.
5. Put the tick in a sealed bag or container, or wrap it in tape. Don't crush it with your fingers.
6. Check the rest of the body, the hair and the clothes. Where there is one tick there may be others.
# Don't
- No petroleum jelly, nail polish, heat or matches. They make the tick push more into the skin.
# Afterwards
- See a doctor if a rash or fever comes in the weeks after. Say when and where the bite happened.
