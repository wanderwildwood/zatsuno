title: Snakebite
keywords: snake, snakebite, rattlesnake, copperhead, cottonmouth, coral snake, venom, bite, antivenom
source: NIOSH (CDC), "Venomous Snakes".
---
! Get to a hospital as soon as you can. Antivenom works best early.
# Do this
1. Move away from the snake. Don't try to catch or kill it; a photo from a safe distance helps.
2. Call 911 or your local emergency number.
3. Keep calm and as still as you can. Don't drive yourself.
4. Lie or sit with the bite in a comfortable, neutral position.
5. Take off rings, watches and tight clothing before swelling starts.
6. Wash the bite with soap and water and cover it with a clean, dry dressing.
7. Mark the edge of the swelling on the skin with a pen, and write the time beside it.
# Don't
- No tourniquet, and no cutting the wound.
- Don't suck out the venom.
- No ice, and don't soak it in water.
- No alcohol, and no aspirin, ibuprofen or naproxen.
- Don't handle the snake, even a dead one.
> NIOSH now says to rest the bite in a neutral position. Older advice was to keep it below the heart.
