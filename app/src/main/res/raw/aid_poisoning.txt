! Several people sick at once near a stove, heater, engine or fire: think carbon monoxide. Get everyone into fresh air now.
! Not awake, a seizure, or trouble breathing: Call 911 or your local emergency number.
  No signal: try 911 anyway (see Calling for help); if it fails, send someone for help.
# Carbon monoxide (CO)
1. Get everyone out into fresh air. Turn off the source only if you can do it fast. Don't go back in.
2. Call 911, even if they start to feel better.
  No signal: try 911 anyway (see Calling for help); if it fails, everyone with signs heads out to care today. Passed out, confused, chest pain or can't walk: send someone for help.
3. Not breathing normally: start CPR. See Hands-only CPR.
# Swallowed poison
- Plants, berries, mushrooms, medicines, fuel or cleaners
1. Call Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222 (US), any time. Very ill: call 911.
  No signal: try 911 anyway (see Calling for help); if it fails, head out to care now, even if they feel fine. Very ill or can't walk: send someone for help.
2. Don't make them vomit. Give nothing by mouth unless told to.
- Swallowed fuel or lamp oil and now coughing, choking or short of breath: Call 911 or your local emergency number.
3. They vomit: roll them onto their side.
- Pills or drugs, and they won't wake or breathe slowly: give naloxone (Narcan) nose spray if anyone has it. Not breathing normally: start CPR. See Hands-only CPR.
4. Bring the plant, mushroom, packet or bottle with them.
# On skin or in eyes
1. Protect your hands (gloves or a bag). Brush off any dry powder first.
2. Take off soaked clothing. Rinse skin or eyes with lots of clean water for at least 15 minutes. Hold the eyelids open.
3. Then call Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222 (US).
  No signal: try 911 anyway (see Calling for help); if it fails, a chemical in the eye, or skin that blisters or burns, heads out to care today.
+++
- It has no smell. Stoves, heaters, charcoal and engines in tents, cabins, vans or snow caves make it.
- Signs: headache, dizziness, weakness, nausea, vomiting, chest pain, confusion. Sleepy or drunk people may not wake.
- Don't go back to sleep near the source.
> Some mushrooms make people sick only 6 hours or more later. Call even if they feel fine.
