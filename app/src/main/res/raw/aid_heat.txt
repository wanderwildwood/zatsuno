# Heat stroke: call 911
- Confusion, slurred speech, strange behavior
- Fainting or collapse, seizures
- Very hot skin, which can be dry or soaked with sweat
1. Call 911 or your local emergency number.
2. Get them into shade and take off outer clothing.
3. Cool them fast. Cold water up to the neck is best if you can. Otherwise soak them and their clothes, and fan hard.
4. Cold packs or wet cloths on the neck, armpits and groin.
5. Stay with them until help arrives.
# Heat exhaustion
- Heavy sweating, headache, dizziness, weakness, nausea, thirst, little urine
1. Get them out of the heat to rest in shade.
2. Give cool water in frequent sips.
3. Loosen or take off extra clothing, shoes and socks. Cool the head, face and neck with cold water.
4. Get medical care. Call 911 if they get worse, become confused, or don't improve within an hour.
> Cold water up to the neck is the fastest way to cool someone with heat stroke, and NIOSH names it first. Every minute of cooling counts.
