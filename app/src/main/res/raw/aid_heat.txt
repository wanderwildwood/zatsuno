# Heat stroke: call 911
- Confusion, slurred speech, strange behavior
- Fainting or collapse, seizures
- Very hot skin, dry or soaked with sweat
1. Call 911 or your local emergency number. No signal: try 911 anyway (see Calling for help); if it fails, start cooling, then send someone.
2. Shade. Take off outer clothing.
3. Cool them fast; every minute counts. Best: cold water up to the neck, head held above it. Otherwise soak them and their clothes and fan hard.
4. Cold packs go on the cheeks, palms and soles of the feet.
5. Keep cooling until they think clearly or start to shiver.
6. They need a hospital even if they seem fine. Stay with them. Don't make them walk out.
# Heat exhaustion
- Heavy sweating, headache, dizziness, weakness, nausea, thirst, little urine
! Confused or fainting: treat as heat stroke and cool them fast.
1. Rest in shade. Remove extra clothing, shoes and socks.
2. Cold water on the head, face and neck.
3. Awake and not vomiting: frequent sips of cool water, plus a sports drink, electrolytes or a salty snack if you have them.
4. Get medical care. Worse, or not better within an hour: call 911 or your local emergency number.
+++
- No fever medicine (aspirin, acetaminophen). No alcohol rubs.
- Nothing by mouth if confused or vomiting.
