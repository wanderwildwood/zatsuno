# A hot spot
- Stop when a spot feels hot or sore. Tape or pad it now, before it blisters.
# A blister
1. Leave it whole if you can. The skin keeps infection out.
  Large, painful or about to burst: wash it, prick the edge with an alcohol-cleaned needle, press out the fluid, leave the skin on. Do not drain it if they have diabetes or poor circulation.
2. Cover it with a bandage, or a pad with a hole cut around it.
3. Fix what is rubbing: wet socks, a loose lace, grit in the boot.
# If it has torn open
1. Wash gently with clean water and soap.
2. Leave the skin flap in place.
3. Cover with a clean, non-stick dressing.
+++
# Get medical care if
- It looks infected: pus, spreading redness or red streaks, warmth, swelling, more pain, fever.
  Fever or red streaks far from help: head out now.
- They have diabetes or poor circulation.
- No clear cause, or many at once.
- From a burn or cold: see Burns or Frostbite.
