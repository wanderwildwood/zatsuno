# A hot spot
- Stop as soon as a spot feels hot or sore. Tape or pad it before a blister forms.
# A blister
1. Leave it whole if you can. The skin over it keeps infection out.
2. Cover it with a bandage or a pad with a hole cut out around the blister.
3. Find what is rubbing and change it: wet socks, a loose lace, grit in the boot.
# If it has torn open
1. Wash it gently with clean water and soap.
2. Leave the flap of skin in place.
3. Cover it with a clean, non-stick dressing.
# Get medical care if
- It shows infection: pus, spreading redness, warmth, swelling, more pain.
- The person has diabetes or poor circulation.
