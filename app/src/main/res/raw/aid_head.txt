title: Head injury
keywords: concussion, skull, fall, hit head, knocked out, unconscious, neck, spine, helmet, vomiting, pupils
source: MedlinePlus (NIH), "Head injury: first aid"; CDC, "HEADS UP: Signs and Symptoms of Concussion".
---
# Call 911 if they
- Were knocked out, even briefly
- Are confused, very drowsy, or can't be woken
- Have a headache that gets worse, or vomit more than once
- Have one pupil larger than the other, slurred speech, weakness or numbness
- Have a seizure, or clear fluid or blood from the nose or ears
# Do this
1. Keep the head and neck still, in line with the body. Assume the neck is hurt too.
2. Check breathing. Start CPR if needed, moving the head as little as you can.
3. If they vomit, roll head, neck and body together onto their side.
4. Bleeding from the scalp: press gently with a cloth. If the skull may be broken, cover the wound and don't press.
# Don't
- Don't move them unless you must.
- Don't take off a helmet if the injury may be serious.
- Don't shake someone who seems dazed.
# After a mild knock
- Someone should stay with them for the next day. Signs can show up hours later.
- No more activity that day, and no alcohol.
