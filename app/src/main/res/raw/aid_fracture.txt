title: Fractures and splints
keywords: broken bone, fracture, dislocation, splint, sling, immobilize, deformed limb, open fracture
source: MedlinePlus (NIH), "Broken bone" and "Splints".
---
! Call 911 if bone shows through the skin, the limb is cold, pale or numb beyond the injury, or the hip, pelvis, thigh, neck or back may be broken.
# Do this
1. Check breathing and stop any heavy bleeding first.
2. Bone through the skin: cover the wound with something clean. Don't push the bone back.
3. Splint it the way you found it. Don't straighten it.
4. Pad the splint and tie it so it holds the joint above and the joint below the break.
5. Check the fingers or toes before and after: warm, pink, and they can feel you touch them. If not, loosen the ties.
6. Cool the area with a wrapped cold pack.
- A splint can be a stick, a rolled map, a sleeping pad, a trekking pole. A sling holds an arm.
# Don't
- Don't move them if the neck, back, hip or pelvis may be hurt, unless where they are is dangerous.
- Don't test whether the bone moves.
