# Treat it as a break if
- It is out of shape, or bone may be showing
- They can't put any weight on it, or can't move the joint
- It is numb, cold or pale beyond the injury, or pain is severe over the bone
- See Fractures and splints.
# For the first two days
1. Rest: keep weight off it.
2. Ice: a cold pack, or snow or cold water in a bag, wrapped in cloth. 15 to 20 minutes, every few hours.
3. Wrap: an elastic wrap, snug but not tight. Fingers or toes numb, tingly, cold or pale: loosen it now.
4. Raise it above the heart when you can.
+++
- No heat, hot soaks, alcohol or rubbing. They add swelling.
# Walking out on it
- A firm wrap or tape helps. Go slowly. Stop if pain gets sharp.
- Can't walk on it, or it gets worse: stop. Splint it, keep them warm, and get help. See Calling for help.
