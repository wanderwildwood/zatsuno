# Do this for the first two days
1. Rest: keep weight off it.
2. Ice: a cold pack wrapped in cloth, 15 to 20 minutes at a time, every few hours.
3. Compression: an elastic wrap, snug but not tight. Fingers or toes should stay warm and pink.
4. Elevation: raise it above the heart when you can.
# Treat it as a break if
- It is out of shape, or the bone may be showing
- They can't put any weight on it, or can't move the joint
- It is numb, or pain is severe over the bone itself
- See Fractures and splints.
# Walking out on it
- A firm wrap or tape gives some support. Go slowly and stop if pain gets sharp.
